Recruitment Process

A general overview of how a recruitment application may progress.

1

Profile Registration

Submit your basic details, experience and preferred employment information through the registration process.

2

Profile Review

Candidate information is reviewed for potentially suitable opportunities.

3

Opportunity Matching

Suitable opportunities may be discussed based on the candidate profile and available openings.

4

Documentation

Required documents and processing requirements are communicated according to the applicable opportunity.

5

Selection

Interviews, employer selection or other assessment stages may apply depending on the opportunity.

6

Joining Support

Where applicable, candidates may receive coordination support for travel and joining procedures.
